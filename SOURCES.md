# Content and asset sources

The Sidearm description follows its owning product documentation. The page presents a project link, not a promise of hosted access or public source availability.

The geometric R, Sidearm bicep card mark, and favicon are authored vector artwork. The geometric R remains provisional pending the separate mascot review.

Self-hosted typefaces come from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License: [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk) and [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope). Their license notices accompany the font files.


The local privacy integration follows Matomo's [JavaScript API](https://developer.matomo.org/guides/tracking-javascript), [asynchronous queue and custom opt-out contract](https://developer.matomo.org/guides/tracking-javascript-guide), and [Heatmap/Session Recording API](https://developer.matomo.org/guides/heatmap-session-recording/reference). Deployment review also uses the [CNIL audience-measurement requirements](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-solutions-pour-les-outils-de-mesure-daudience); the site makes no automatic exemption claim. Matomo server configuration and activation receipts belong to Org-Rename.
